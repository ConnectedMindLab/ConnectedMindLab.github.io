## What is ConnectedMind Cognitive Profiling?

ConnectedMind Cognitive Profiling is not simply a test to determine whether there is a "problem" or not.
It is a process of comprehensively understanding a person's cognitive strengths and vulnerabilities, emotional state, and neurophysiological patterns to design an optimized training plan for the individual.

From developmental challenges in children and adolescents to burnout, sleep problems, social anxiety, and reduced focus in adults — regardless of age, we help you deeply understand who you are today and explore the path forward together.

---

## Assessment Areas

### Cognitive & Executive Function
- Intelligence Test (K-WISC-V / K-WAIS): memory, problem-solving, processing speed
- CAT Comprehensive Attention Test: selective, sustained, and divided attention; inhibitory control
- Stroop Test, Trail Making Test: executive function, cognitive flexibility

### Neurophysiological Evaluation
- QEEG Brain Function Assessment: brainwave pattern analysis, target frequency setting for training

### Emotion & Personality
- MMPI-2 (adults) / MMPI-A (adolescents): personality and mental health assessment
- Temperament and Character Inventory (TCI): dispositional characteristics
- Anxiety & Depression Scales: emotional dysregulation assessment

### Children & Adolescents — Additional Areas
- Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL, K-BASC)
- Learning style assessment, reading assessment
- Parent interview and school adjustment evaluation

---

## Assessment Process

- **Duration**: 2–3 hours (may be split into 2 sessions if needed)
- **Target**: Children aged 5 and above through adults
- **Method**: 1:1 individual assessment with a specialist, questionnaires, interview
- **Report**: Comprehensive interpretive report and feedback consultation within 1–2 weeks after completion

---

## Recommended For

**Children & Adolescents**
- When attention problems, impulse control issues, or academic underperformance are a concern
- When emotional difficulties such as anxiety, depression, or anger are recurring
- When you want an objective assessment of developmental strengths and potential
- When school life or peer relationships are difficult

**Adults**
- When chronic fatigue or burnout is affecting daily performance
- When sleep problems or arousal regulation difficulties persist
- When social anxiety, emotional dysregulation, or relationship exhaustion is ongoing
- When reduced focus or memory decline is a concern
- When you want an objective understanding of your cognitive and emotional profile
