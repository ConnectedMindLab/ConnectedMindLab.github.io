## What is an Intelligence Assessment?

An intelligence assessment is not simply a test to confirm an IQ number.
It analyzes a detailed cognitive profile — including verbal comprehension, visuospatial processing, fluid reasoning, working memory, and processing speed —
to objectively identify an individual's cognitive strengths and vulnerabilities.

---

## Assessment Instruments

### Children & Adolescents
**K-WISC-V** (Ages 6 years to 16 years 11 months)
- Verbal Comprehension Index: vocabulary, verbal reasoning ability
- Visual Spatial Index: visual information processing, spatial construction ability
- Fluid Reasoning Index: logical thinking, problem-solving ability
- Working Memory Index: ability to retain and manipulate information
- Processing Speed Index: cognitive processing speed and accuracy

### Adults
**K-WAIS-IV** (Ages 16 to 69)
- Verbal Comprehension Index: vocabulary, general knowledge, comprehension
- Perceptual Reasoning Index: visual problem-solving, spatial processing
- Working Memory Index: attention, short-term memory, mental manipulation
- Processing Speed Index: visual-motor coordination, processing speed

---

## Assessment Overview

- **Duration**: 60–90 minutes
- **Target**: Children aged 6 and above through adults
- **Method**: 1:1 individual assessment with a specialist
- **Report**: Comprehensive interpretive report and feedback consultation within 1–2 weeks after completion

---

## Recommended For

- When you want to understand the cognitive causes behind academic underperformance
- When cognitive decline or burnout in adults is a concern
- When a baseline cognitive profile is needed before neurofeedback or cognitive training
