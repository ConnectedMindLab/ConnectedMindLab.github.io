## Neurofeedback Equipment

Real-time brainwave measurement via scalp electrodes provides audiovisual feedback when target frequency bands are achieved, strengthening the brain's self-regulation capacity.

### How It Works

1. Scalp electrodes record real-time EEG signals
2. Activation of target frequency bands (e.g., SMR, alpha, theta) is analyzed in real time
3. Positive feedback (visual/auditory) is provided when the target is achieved
4. Repeated training improves the brain's self-regulatory ability

### Applications

- ADHD and reduced attention
- Anxiety and hyperarousal regulation
- Sleep quality improvement
- Peak Performance training
