## Gymtop Sensory Integration Training

Integrative training for balance, coordination, and sensory processing, improving body regulation in children with sensory processing difficulties.

### Training Principles

1. Activities integrating multiple sensory inputs (vestibular, proprioceptive, tactile)
2. Balance maintenance, coordination, and sensory discrimination training
3. Strengthens sensory-motor integration circuits between brain and body
4. Builds the foundation for self-regulation and attention

### Applications

- Children with sensory processing difficulties
- Delayed balance and coordination development
- Co-occurring ADHD and sensory processing issues
- Need for improved body awareness and motor regulation
