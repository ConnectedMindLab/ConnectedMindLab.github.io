## ADHD & Neurodevelopmental Training Program

Train attention regulation, impulse control, and executive function — with or without medication.

Through neurofeedback, Interactive Metronome, and sensory integration, we strengthen the brain's self-regulation capacity and aim for improved performance in daily life and academics.

### Training Modalities

- **Neurofeedback**: Real-time EEG feedback for self-regulation of target frequency bands
- **Interactive Metronome**: Auditory timing training to improve attention, sequencing, and executive function
- **Sensory Integration**: Balance, coordination, and sensory processing training

### Target Population

- Children and adolescents diagnosed with ADHD
- Those seeking non-pharmacological or combined training
- Individuals needing improvement in attention, impulsivity, and executive function

All programs are individually designed based on prior assessment and consultation.
