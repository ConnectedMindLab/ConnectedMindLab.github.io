## Gifted Children Peak Performance Program

We analyze and optimize cognitive profiles so that high potential translates into actual performance.

Performance in music and mathematics is not merely about knowledge accumulation — it is a function of flow state entry, arousal regulation, error recovery, and emotional-cognitive control.

### Training Modalities

- **Neurofeedback**: Flow state (theta/alpha) entry and maintenance training
- **Biofeedback (HRV)**: Autonomic nervous system balance and pre-performance arousal regulation
- **Peak Performance Protocol**: Individually designed based on cognitive profile

### Target Population

- Gifted children and adolescents in music and mathematics
- Those with high potential but underperforming
- Individuals experiencing test anxiety, performance anxiety, or perfectionism

All programs are individually designed based on prior assessment and consultation.
