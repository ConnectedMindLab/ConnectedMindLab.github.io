## Adult & Professional Peak Performance Program

Enhance focus, stress regulation, and decision-making ability for working professionals.

Chronic stress, burnout, sleep problems, and decision fatigue are closely linked to reduced regulatory capacity of the brain and autonomic nervous system.

### Training Modalities

- **HRV Biofeedback**: Real-time heart rate variability monitoring, autonomic balance and stress resilience training
- **Neurofeedback**: Focus, cognitive flexibility, and emotional regulation training
- **tVNS (Vagus Nerve Stimulation)**: Parasympathetic activation, relaxation and recovery enhancement

### Target Population

- Professionals experiencing burnout or chronic stress
- Those with reduced focus and decision fatigue
- Individuals seeking improved sleep quality and resilience

All programs are individually designed based on prior assessment and consultation.
