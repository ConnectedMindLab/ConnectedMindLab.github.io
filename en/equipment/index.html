Biofeedback HRV

Biofeedback HRV (Heart Rate Variability)

Real-time monitoring of heart rate variability (HRV) to train autonomic nervous system balance, improving stress resilience and emotional regulation.

How It Works

  1. ECG or PPG sensor measures cardiac signals
  2. HRV is analyzed in real time
  3. Breathing and relaxation guidance trains autonomic resonance frequency
  4. Improves sympathetic-parasympathetic balance and stress recovery

Applications

  • Chronic stress and burnout
  • Performance anxiety and test anxiety
  • Emotional dysregulation
  • Holistic mind-body optimization

Gymtop Sensory Integration

Gymtop Sensory Integration Training

Integrative training for balance, coordination, and sensory processing, improving body regulation in children with sensory processing difficulties.

Training Principles

  1. Activities integrating multiple sensory inputs (vestibular, proprioceptive, tactile)
  2. Balance maintenance, coordination, and sensory discrimination training
  3. Strengthens sensory-motor integration circuits between brain and body
  4. Builds the foundation for self-regulation and attention

Applications

  • Children with sensory processing difficulties
  • Delayed balance and coordination development
  • Co-occurring ADHD and sensory processing issues
  • Need for improved body awareness and motor regulation

Neurofeedback

Neurofeedback Equipment

Real-time brainwave measurement via scalp electrodes provides audiovisual feedback when target frequency bands are achieved, strengthening the brain’s self-regulation capacity.

How It Works

  1. Scalp electrodes record real-time EEG signals
  2. Activation of target frequency bands (e.g., SMR, alpha, theta) is analyzed in real time
  3. Positive feedback (visual/auditory) is provided when the target is achieved
  4. Repeated training improves the brain’s self-regulatory ability

Applications

  • ADHD and reduced attention
  • Anxiety and hyperarousal regulation
  • Sleep quality improvement
  • Peak Performance training

tVNS (Transcutaneous Vagus Nerve Stimulation)

tVNS — Transcutaneous Vagus Nerve Stimulation

Micro-current delivered to the skin around the ear (auricular region) stimulates the vagus nerve and activates the parasympathetic nervous system.

How It Works

  1. Small electrodes attached to the auricular region of the ear
  2. Gentle electrical stimulation travels via the auricular branch of the vagus nerve to the brainstem
  3. Activates parasympathetic nervous system; modulates norepinephrine and acetylcholine
  4. Promotes relaxation and recovery; enhances neuroplasticity

Applications

  • Hyperarousal and chronic stress
  • Attention and focus enhancement
  • Pre-session preparation for neurofeedback
  • Cases requiring parasympathetic activation
